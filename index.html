The Problem

One QR code, many vendors, zero records

"While purchasing goods in a local market in Ajmer, a vendor gestured towards a shop across the road and said 'Uspe kardo' — do it on that one. The payment went to someone else's QR code. There was zero record of which vendor had received what amount. That moment became UsPe."

How It Works

Four steps, zero change for the customer

1

Create vendor profile

The QR owner adds each vendor's name — UsPe generates a unique QR code per vendor automatically.

2

Print & display QR

Each vendor displays their own printed QR. Customers scan and pay exactly as before, on PhonePe, GPay, Paytm or BHIM.

3

Auto attribution

UsPe reads the incoming UPI notification, with SMS reading and manual assign as backup, and matches it to the correct vendor.

4

Daily settlement

An end-of-day dashboard shows exactly what each vendor collected. The QR owner pays out and the ledger updates instantly.
